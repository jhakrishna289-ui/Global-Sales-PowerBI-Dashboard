# Global Sales & Logistics Performance Analytics Suite

## 📌 Project Overview
Developed an end-to-end Power BI analytics solution for a global retail logistics dataset. The suite provides executives with actionable insights into global revenue distribution, product pipeline optimization, and supply chain risk factors.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Demonstrated
- **Data Engineering & ETL:** Python (Pandas) for initial data hygiene and type casting.
- **Data Modeling:** Power BI Desktop, Star Schema configuration.
- **Analytical Calculations:** Advanced DAX measures for core KPIs (Total Sales, Dynamic Fulfillment & Disputed Rates).
- **UI/UX Dashboard Design:** Clean, modern corporate layout focusing on accessibility and intuitive data slicing.

## 📊 Executive Dashboard Architecture
### Page 1: Global Operations Overview
- **Key Metrics Tracking:** Executive KPI Cards representing Total Revenue ($8.29M), Volume (307 unique orders), and Average Order Values.
- **Temporal Analysis:** Granular time-series charts identifying historic seasonality peaks and pipeline drops.
- **Geospatial Mapping:** Interactive map pinpointing core revenue centers (USA, France, Spain).

### Page 2: Customer Segments & Risk Portfolio
- **Deal Size Stratification:** Comprehensive donut chart isolating revenue shares by micro, medium, and macro deal sizes.
- **Cross-Segment Distribution:** Clustered column charts assessing how specific product lines behave across shifting deal thresholds.

## 💡 Key Business Insights Delivered
1. **Supply Chain Risk Mitigation:** Identified localized friction points in specific European territories showing an elevated 'Disputed Order' rate, giving supply chain managers exact locations for third-party logistics (3PL) reviews.
2. **Product Inventory Alignment:** Classic and Vintage cars emerged as the primary business drivers, capturing the highest volume of high-margin 'Large' and 'Medium' deal sizes. Recommendation delivered to align procurement focus heavily with this high-yield sector.
